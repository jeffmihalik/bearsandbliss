Weather & packing

August afternoons in the mountains bring quick thunderstorms and it's noticeably cooler at altitude than back home. Layers, a rain jacket, sturdy shoes, and sun protection all earn their space in the suitcase.

National Park heads up

Rocky Mountain National Park requires a timed entry reservation to get in from 9am–2pm through mid-October. Reservations for August are already open on Recreation.gov — the sooner someone books, the better the odds. See the Things to Do tab for details.

Things to Do

Suggestions — not editable, just for planning

Book ahead: Rocky Mountain National Park Timed Entry Required

A timed entry reservation is required to enter the park between 9am and 2pm (you can enter before 9am without one). Reservations are booked on Recreation.gov, not at the gate, and August dates are already released — last-minute spots also open at 7pm the night before if you miss the initial booking window. Worth grabbing these early since they sell out.

In the park

  • Trail Ridge Road12,183 ft
    The highest continuous paved highway in the U.S. — incredible views, worth a drive even without getting out much.
  • Bear Lake
    Easy, scenic loop trail around the lake. Needs its own separate Bear Lake Road permit in addition to park entry.
  • Sprague Lake
    Flat, easy, wheelchair/stroller-friendly loop — a good option for a relaxed morning.
  • Alberta Falls
    A short, popular hike to a waterfall, good for most fitness levels.

In town

  • Estes Park Riverwalk
    Shops, ice cream, and taffy along the river — easy, low-key evening activity.
  • The Stanley Hotel
    Historic hotel known as the inspiration for "The Shining" — offers walking tours.
  • Lake Estes
    Paved path around the lake, plus paddleboat and kayak rentals at the marina.
  • Mini golf & go-karts
    A few family fun centers around town — good backup for a rainy afternoon.

A little more adventurous

Horseback rides through nearby stables, fly fishing on the Big Thompson River, and whitewater rafting trips are all offered by outfitters in the area if anyone wants to book something extra.
